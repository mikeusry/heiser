# The Heiser Group — Realtor Tri-Fold Brochure

## Purpose

A tri-fold brochure targeted at **realtors and residential one-time
deep clean prospects**. Handed to realtors in person after David's
first meeting or conversation, and emailed as a PDF follow-up.

## Approach

**Refactor of the existing tri-fold template.** Reuse the entire design
system (diagonal shapes, navy `#1A2B3D` + orange `#E95324`, HG logo,
van photo, existing typography). New copy targeted specifically at
realtors and one-time residential.

The designer opens the existing `.ai` file, keeps the layout skeleton,
and swaps in the copy blocks below panel-by-panel.

---

## Fold Direction & Panel Map

The existing brochure is a **right-fold / letter-fold / C-fold**
(8.5" × 11" landscape). Panels are **not** equal. The flap that
tucks in is 1/16" narrower so the piece sits flat. Measurements
from the printer templates `B_11x8.5 outside.pdf` /
`B_11x8.5 inside.pdf`:

### Front PDF (OUTSIDE — what the recipient sees first)

When flat, reads left→right as three panels. When folded, the RIGHT
panel becomes the cover, the LEFT panel tucks inside, and the MIDDLE
panel is the back.

```
┌──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
│  PANEL A     │  PANEL B     │  PANEL C     │
│  INSIDE FLAP │  OUTSIDE     │  OUTSIDE     │
│  (tucks in)  │  BACK        │  COVER       │
│  Values      │  Contact/QR  │  HG logo +   │
│              │              │  hook line   │
└──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┘
```

### Back PDF (INSIDE — the 3-panel spread the recipient sees when they open it)

When the recipient unfolds the brochure, they see all three panels at
once as a landscape spread.

```
┌──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
│  PANEL D     │  PANEL E     │  PANEL F     │
│  INSIDE      │  INSIDE      │  INSIDE      │
│  LEFT        │  CENTER      │  RIGHT       │
│  For         │  Team + Van  │  What Sets   │
│  Realtors    │  + About     │  Us Apart    │
└──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┘
```

## Print Specs

- 8.5" × 11" landscape, letter-fold (right-fold / C-fold)
- Two-sided (outside + inside)
- 0.125" bleed all sides
- 0.125" safety from trim **and** from each fold
- CMYK for print, or check with printer

**Do not use three equal 3.667" columns.** That is why the proof
folds were off. Lock columns to the printer templates:

| Side | Fold 1 (from left trim) | Fold 2 | Panels L→R |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Outside (Front)** | 3.6246" (260.97 pt) | 7.3103" (526.34 pt) | 3.625" flap \| 3.686" back \| 3.690" cover |
| **Inside (Back)** | 3.6871" (265.47 pt) | 7.3733" (530.88 pt) | 3.687" left \| 3.686" center \| 3.627" right (flap) |

Templates live in OneDrive `2026 Heiser Trifold/printer-templates/`.
